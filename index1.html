Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India & People's President

Biography

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. His dedication to science and education made him a beloved public figure.

He played a key role in India's missile and space programmes. Because of his contributions, he became popularly known as the "Missile Man of India."

Even after serving as President, he continued inspiring students through lectures and books. He believed that young minds have the power to transform the nation.

His life continues to motivate millions through his simplicity, honesty, hard work, and vision for a developed India.

Major Achievements

  • 1960 – Joined DRDO as a scientist.
  • 1969 – Joined ISRO and worked on India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle.
  • 1998 – Played an important role in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
  • 2002 – Became the 11th President of India.
  • 2015 – Passed away while delivering a lecture to students.
